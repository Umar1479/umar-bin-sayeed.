How I protect your programme

Four steps, one answer to each worry. Clients tell us the same things. Handover dates slip. Trades are not coordinated. Progress is hard to see. Approvals hold the work. My method answers each one.

1

Plan

Worry: delays

A baseline built from scope, WBS, productivity, procurement and your contract milestones.

  • Primavera P6 baseline
  • Cost- and resource-loaded
  • Procurement and approvals in the logic
2

Monitor

Worry: approvals and cost overruns

Critical path checks against the baseline every update. Late approvals and procurement are tracked.

  • CPM variance analysis
  • Submittal and shop-drawing trackers
  • Earned value: SPI and CPI
3

Report

Worry: visibility

Short, regular reports for the client, the consultant and our management. The same facts for everyone.

  • Weekly and monthly reports
  • Look-ahead programme
  • KPI dashboard
4

Recover

Worry: missed handover

When a date is at risk, I raise it early, show the options and protect the contract position.

  • Early-warning and delay notices
  • Recovery and acceleration plans
  • EOT support with records

AI Planning Copilot

In development, not yet in use on client projects

I am building a Planning Engineer AI Copilot at Motive. It does the repetitive part of planning work. I keep the judgement and sign-off.

Weekly XER analysis

Compares each Primavera P6 update with the baseline. Flags slippage, float loss and new critical activities. Drafts the weekly report and refreshes the dashboard data.

BOQ cost and resource loading

Maps the priced BOQ to activities and loads cost and resources. Totals are reconciled to the BOQ so every update can earn value.

Tender technical submissions

Reads the RFP, builds the compliance matrix and drafts the methodology and programme sections from Motive's own library.

Rules it follows

  • Code does the arithmetic. AI reads and writes.
  • It never invents data. Missing information goes on a gap list.
  • Every output keeps its source.
  • A person reviews and signs before any notice, EOT claim or submission.
